都江堰｜六人英文排练本（不上屏）
展示 4:00（含 16 秒小组配乐），六人每人都讲英语；问答另计 1 分钟。
只用一张海报。线上打开课堂页；离线解压后用 Edge / Chrome 打开 index.html，点 Full screen。
方括号是动作提示，不读；段落时间是排练目标。尽量每段留 1–2 秒指图、停顿或交接。

SPEAKER 1 · 0:00–0:37 | The problem
[从鱼嘴水墨实景开场。指标题下方的问题。]
Imagine you are a farmer by the Min River. On dry days, your fields need water. After heavy rain, the same river may flood them. So how do you water the fields without blocking the river? Dujiangyan answers this question by giving water different paths. Let us follow those paths on the map.

SPEAKER 2 · 0:37–1:14 | Fish Mouth
[按 1。先指来水，再沿内江和外江各指一次。]
At number one, the river meets a long divider called Fish Mouth. It splits the water into two channels. The outer river keeps flowing downstream. The inner river carries water toward the fields. But if too much water enters the inner river, we need to give it a way out. That brings us to number two.

SPEAKER 3 · 1:14–1:51 | Flying Sand Weir
[按 2。沿短箭头指向外江。]
This is Flying Sand Weir. A weir is a low wall in a river. When the inner river rises high enough, extra water flows over the wall and back into the outer river. It can carry sand away, too. This helps keep the channel clear. Now let us see how water enters the canals.

SPEAKER 4 · 1:51–2:28 | Bottle Neck
[按 3。先指窄口，再指成都平原方向。]
Number three is Bottle Neck. The opening is narrow, like the neck of a bottle. It limits how much water can enter the canals. The canals then carry the water to fields across the Chengdu Plain. Together, the weir and the narrow opening give extra water a way out and give farms a steady supply.

SPEAKER 5 · 2:28–3:05 | Work with nature
[按 0 回到水墨实景，指下方的「道法自然」。]
Our poster uses a line from the Dao De Jing: “The Way follows nature.” For us, Dujiangyan is a clear example. The engineers used the shape and slope of the river to guide water. They did not need to block the whole river. The fields get the water they need, and extra water can keep flowing downstream.

SPEAKER 6 · 3:05–4:00 | Hear the river
[留在实景；说完第一段再放 16 秒小组配乐。]
Listen to our short piece, River in Two. You will hear one rhythm first and then a second one. As you listen, think about the point where the Min River splits.

[约 3:28 播放；16 秒内不说话，约 3:44 接回总结。]

The two rhythms stand for the two channels at Fish Mouth. Our example shows that good design can work with nature. Thank you.


4:00–5:00 | Q&A：展示结束后另计。听完老师问题再答；不要连读三题。
Q1: What happens when the inner river rises too high?
A1: Some of the extra water flows over Flying Sand Weir and back into the outer river. The water can carry sand away, too.

Q2: How does Bottle Neck help the farms?
A2: The narrow opening limits how much water enters the canals. The canals then carry the water to the fields.

Q3: Why did your group use two rhythms?
A3: The first rhythm stands for the Min River. The second joins to help people hear how the river splits into two channels.

没听清：Could you repeat the question, please?

小组自制音乐｜一条河，两种节奏
采用现场创作和演奏，不播放旧 Demo 或现成歌曲。排练时由组员定下自己的两个节奏和收尾音；以下是可改的谱架。每格一拍，速度约每分钟 90 拍，六小节共 16 秒。× 为轻敲，— 为休止。先轻声数一小节再开始（正式表演不数出声）。
第1–2小节：S1 独奏桌面低音，例 × — × —。其余人静听。
第3–4小节：S1 继续；S2 用两支笔敲出不同节奏，例 — × × —。S3 在每小节第四拍轻拍手，像水越过低堰。
第5小节：S1、S2、S3 保持节奏，S4 轻哼两个自选音，示意水流入田。
第6小节：全组在第一拍合奏，余下三拍静默。可改最后一拍，保留“一变二”的结构。
课前由全组决定节奏和收尾音。可在 Edit score 编曲，再点 Save WAV 导出小组版；也可自己合奏并录音。示范版 River-in-Two-Demo.wav 不当作小组作品。

排练提醒
按秒表连跑两遍。第一遍只对交接和按键；第二遍把完整音频算入 4:00。给配乐留足 16 秒。
约 90–100 词/分钟是排练参考。若超时，优先去掉额外停顿；若还超时，第 1 人可省去第 2 句、第 4 人可省去瓶颈比喻。不要删任何人的整段。
提示：weir（近 wear）= 低堰；downstream = 下游；slope = 坡度；canal = 水渠。
