# Dujiangyan · The Min River Scroll
[Open the live classroom poster](https://wcnz-coder.github.io/dujiangyan-river-atlas/).

A single English teaching poster. The opening landscape is an imagined ink painting; the separate annotated flow diagram explains the actual engineering. Click **Unroll painting** to see the complete uncropped artwork. Press **1–3** to trace Fish Mouth, Flying Sand Weir and Bottle Neck. Press **0** to return to the landscape.

**Classroom use:** Full screen → present for five minutes (including music) → one minute of Q&A. Notes and the four-speaker script are available on the poster and in Rehearsal.txt. Edit score to choose the group's rhythm and final note, then copy the URL including its fragment to the classroom machine. The default 16-second score is a model, not a student recording.

**Offline:** extract the whole package, retaining assets/, and open index.html. No build, server, network fonts or external audio needed.

**Credits:** original AI-generated ink landscape, illustrative rather than a historical reproduction. Calligraphy type: Ma Shan Zheng (SIL OFL; license in assets/). Historical reference: UNESCO World Heritage Centre, Mount Qingcheng and the Dujiangyan Irrigation System.
